Helpful reminders

  • Read the whole sentence before choosing a tense.
  • Look for time clues such as yesterday, since, for, by Friday, or this time tomorrow.
  • Check the whole verb phrase, including the auxiliary and the main verb form.
  • If you get an answer wrong, use the explanation and try again. Mistakes help you decide what to practise.
